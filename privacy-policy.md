
# Privacy Policy

**Sure2Take** (the "app")
Effective date: September 30, 2026

The app is published by Raiza Rehkoff, an individual ("we," "us"). You can reach us at **healthy2date@gmail.com**.

This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1\. Information we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and password. We never see your password in readable form. Our authentication provider stores it in protected form.

**Health-related information you enter.** Medication and supplement names, strength, dose, form, directions, prescriber, pharmacy, quantity remaining, refill and expiration dates, your reminder schedules, and your dose history.

**Profile information.** Your display name (optional) and your time zone.

**Stored on your device.** Reminders scheduled on your iPhone, and a record that you acknowledged the medical disclaimer.

**Technical information.** Our hosting provider may log basic technical data, such as IP address and request details, to run and secure the service.

**What we do not collect.** We do not collect your location, contacts, or photos. If we add features such as label scanning, we will update this policy before they launch.

## 2\. How we use your information

* To create and secure your account.
* To store your medications and schedules and to send you reminders.
* To respond to support requests.
* To protect the service and follow the law.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3\. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the app:

* **Supabase**: database hosting, authentication, and server functions.
* **Apple**: App Store distribution and subscription payments. Apple processes your payment. We do not receive your card details.
* **An email delivery provider**: to send account emails, such as confirmation and password reset messages.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or if the app is sold or transferred to another owner. In that case we would tell you first.

Your reminders are scheduled on your device. Depending on your iPhone settings, a reminder may show a medication name on your lock screen. You can change this in your iPhone notification settings.

## 4\. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed on servers operated by our providers, which may be in the United States or other countries. Our main database is hosted in the West US (Oregon) region. If you live outside the United States, using the app means your information will be transferred to and processed in those countries.

## 5\. Consent for health information

Medication information is sensitive. By creating an account and entering medication information, you consent to our processing it to provide the app. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a similar region, this is your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

## 6\. Retention and deletion

We keep your information until you delete it or delete your account.

You can delete your account inside the app: **Profile → Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your medications, schedules, dose history, profile, and login, and removes the app's reminders from your iPhone. Copies in backups may remain for up to 7 days before they are erased.

If you cannot use the app, email us and we will delete your data.

## 7\. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email **healthy2date@gmail.com**. We will respond within the time the law requires. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8\. Security

We use safeguards such as encrypted connections and access controls that limit each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9\. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

## 10\. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a significant change, we will notify you in the app or by email. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
